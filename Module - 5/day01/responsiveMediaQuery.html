Access to credit has a transformative power to drive positive change and empower individuals and businesses in underserved communities. It is not just a financial tool; it's a catalyst for growth, innovation, and the realization of dreams. At Northern Arc, our mission is to cater to the diverse retail credit requirements of the under-served households and businesses.

Over the last 15 years, we have created a differentiated and comprehensive play on the retail credit ecosystem in India and have developed domain expertise in enabling credit across our six focused sectors- MSME financing, Microfinance, Consumer Finance, vehicle finance (commercial vehicle, two-wheeler and EV finance), affordable housing finance and Agriculture finance.

In addition, we are focused on the climate and renewable financing sector. Our business is supported by our proprietary end-to-end integrated technology product suite customised to multiple sectors.
